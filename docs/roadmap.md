# Roadmap and evidence gaps

## Delivered vertical slice

Primary-source retrieval with hashes; all 36 India state/UT groundwater rows; reviewed headline claims; Python and JS consumption accounting; conditional futures; public interactive UI; SQL views and analysis; Excel audit workbook; Power BI build pack.

## Next research milestone

One facility/basin case study with reliable operational period, IT/facility energy, direct water definition, documented water source and local supply context. Require source-specific reuse permission before redistributing data. Prefer a small defensible study over an unsupported world ranking.

## Ten-topic coverage

| Topic | v0.1 status | Gap before quantitative expansion |
|---|---|---|
| Direct cooling | General accounting + illustrative scenarios | Site consumptive WUE and period |
| Electricity water | User-input accounting | Region/cooling-mix intensity and supply allocation |
| Semiconductor manufacturing | Out of quantitative scope | Fab/chip/lifetime allocation and matching definitions |
| Siting and water stress | India state context; WRI method context | Facility supply and basin mapping |
| Heat and drought | Descriptive limitation | Local seasonal climate, load and water data |
| Competing demand | Questions and source context | Shared-source quantities and causal design |
| Quality and discharge | Not modeled | Permit/effluent observations and definitions |
| Disclosure | Typed sources and boundary caveats | Compatible multi-company time series |
| Inference and rebound | Scoped serving study + demand/efficiency sandbox | Representative task mix and observed demand |
| Water-energy-carbon | Interactive model with assumptions | Calibrated feasible technology bundles |

## Release discipline

Do not replace missing facility data with plausible coordinates, capacities or cooling factors. Do not announce model scenarios as measured savings. A world map, ranking or forecast requires an evidence and validation milestone of its own.
