What can be done

Companies: disclose comparable boundaries, test whole-system cooling choices, reduce energy per useful result, monitor seasonality and keep replenishment separate. Governments: request supply and drought plans and monitor discharge. Communities: ask for local supply and competing-use evidence. Individuals: choose adequate tools and share sourced claims with caveats; quantified personal water savings remain a gap.

Limits

No global AI water estimate, precise facility/basin overlay, independent community causal study, chip-water allocation, validated PBIX or confirmed Excel Desktop run. PostgreSQL execution status is recorded separately. Regional and company evidence do not establish local damage. The historical limitations file is not silently upgraded before author confirmation.
