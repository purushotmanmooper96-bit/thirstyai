# Limits of v0.1

- No measured facility water or capacity records. No global AI water total or validated hotspot ranking.
- India dataset: state/UT groundwater, annual all-use flows, 2025. No AI extraction attribution or facility supply mapping.
- Scenario controls and defaults are illustrative. They are not technology calibration, feasibility constraints, forecasts or savings achieved.
- No country-calibrated grid water dataset or power-plant supply allocation. Grid factors are user assumptions.
- No lifecycle chip/fab water allocation, quantitative water-quality model, or community causal study.
- No automatic guarantee that a newly changed report remains semantically comparable; human review is required.
- The public web app, Python engine and SQLite views are implemented. PostgreSQL SQL is supplied but has not been executed against a PostgreSQL server here.
- Power BI is a build pack, not a tested PBIX. Excel formulas are checked in the authoring engine; Microsoft Excel Desktop is not available here.
- Independent source reproduction is not independent assurance of underlying company data.
- There are no live sensors, hourly weather integration, live facility monitoring, recommendations certified for engineering use, or machine-learning forecasts.
