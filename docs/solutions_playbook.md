# Solutions and maintenance

The app's Solutions page is the public summary. Each intervention needs a baseline, period, feasible configuration and monitored outcome before a savings claim.

| Actor | Action | What to monitor | Important limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Operator | Compare heat rejection choices | PUE, defined consumptive WUE, seasonal peak | Chip-side liquid cooling alone does not identify final water use |
| Operator | Investigate reclaimed supply | Freshwater share, consumption, treatment energy | Reclaimed water can still be consumed |
| Operator | Improve useful-work efficiency | Task quality, energy per task, total energy | Demand growth can outweigh efficiency |
| Regulator / community | Request comparable disclosure | Source, period, withdrawal, consumption, permits | Missing reporting does not prove misconduct |
| Siting team | Investigate actual supply and drought | Basin/assessment unit, supply reliability, competing users | State averages are not site assessments |
| Research maintainer | Refresh primary sources | Hashes, locators, definitions, coverage | Automated anchors do not establish semantic comparability |

Cost or liters-saved-per-dollar rankings are withheld without comparable capital, operating, lifetime and baseline evidence. Geographic shifting needs latency, grid and destination-water accounting. Replenishment remains a separate ledger with place/time/additionality questions. No unsupported universal recommendation is made for individual chatbot users.

Research context: MS24 (cooling design/energy trade-offs); GOOGLE25 (serving efficiency and scoped water allocation); WRI23 (screening and local investigation); CGWB25 (all-use groundwater assessment). See research/sources.json for links and locators.
